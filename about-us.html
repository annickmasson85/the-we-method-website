Our Story

Born From a Dream
By the Water.

The We Method was born from a dream of building something meaningful by the water. What started as a family journey unexpectedly became a business opportunity when we acquired PaddlePOC, a rental company in Port O’Connor, Texas.

As we expanded into golf cart rentals, we experienced firsthand what it truly takes to build a successful rental business.

That experience became the foundation of The We Method.

The People Behind the Method

From Our Family to Yours.

◆

We did not arrive in Port O’Connor looking for a business. We were a family from Canada, simply traveling together, when this small town by the water unexpectedly found its way into our story.

Port O’Connor made us imagine a life we had never planned. We made the decision to apply for an E-2 visa and step toward a future we could not yet fully see. There were no guarantees—only our family, an unexpected place, and one life-changing question:

What if this could become our home?

The We Method was born from that leap of faith. It is our way of holding the door open for the dreamers who are still standing on the other side, wondering whether the life they imagine could ever become real.

From our family to yours, we want you to know that sometimes the place you find by accident becomes the place where everything begins.
